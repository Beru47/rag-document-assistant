# Leave Policy

Full-time employees receive 25 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Up to five unused days may be carried over into the next year, and they expire on March 31.

Parental leave lasts 16 weeks at full pay for both parents. It can be taken in one block or split into two periods within the first year after birth or adoption.

A doctor's note is required once sick leave exceeds three consecutive days. Employees should notify their manager before 09:30 on the first day of absence.
