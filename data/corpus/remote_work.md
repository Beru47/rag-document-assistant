# Remote Work Policy

Orbis Logistics employees may work remotely up to three days per week. Teams agree on their office days at the start of each quarter, and managers may ask for more office presence during launches.

Everyone must be reachable during core hours, which run from 10:00 to 15:00 Central European Time. Outside core hours people may arrange their day freely as long as agreed deliverables are met.

New remote workers receive a one-time home-office stipend of 400 EUR for a desk, chair or monitor. Staff who prefer a coworking space can claim reimbursement of up to 120 EUR per month with a valid invoice.
