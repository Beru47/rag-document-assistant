# Incident Response Runbook

Incidents are classified by severity. For a SEV1 incident the on-call engineer must acknowledge the page within 15 minutes and open a dedicated incident channel.

The on-call rotation is weekly, and the hand-over happens every Monday at 09:00. During a SEV1 the public status page is updated every 30 minutes until service is restored.

A written postmortem is due within five business days of resolution. Postmortems are blameless and must list concrete follow-up actions with owners.
