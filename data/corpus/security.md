# Information Security Standard

Passwords must contain at least 14 characters and may not be reused across systems. Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for all internal tools, and administrators must use hardware security keys.

All company laptops use full disk encryption, and lost devices must be reported immediately so they can be wiped remotely. Personal USB storage devices are blocked by default.

Any suspected security incident must be reported to security@orbis.example within one hour of discovery. Access rights to production systems are reviewed every quarter by the system owners.
