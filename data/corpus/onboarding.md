# Onboarding Guide

Laptops are shipped to new hires three days before their start date so that accounts can be activated on day one. Each new hire is paired with an onboarding buddy for the entire first week.

The standard probation period lasts six months. During this time either side may end the contract with a shortened notice period of two weeks.

Managers prepare a 30-60-90 day plan for every newcomer and review progress with them at day 30. Mandatory security training must be completed within the first ten working days.
