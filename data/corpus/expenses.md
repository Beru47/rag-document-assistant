# Travel and Expense Policy

Expense reports must be submitted within 30 days of the purchase date. Reports filed later are only reimbursed with written approval from the finance controller.

Receipts are required for every purchase above 15 EUR. During business trips the meal allowance is 25 EUR for lunch and 45 EUR for dinner. Alcohol is never reimbursed.

Economy class is the default for all flights. Flights longer than six hours may be booked in business class, but only after a vice president has approved the booking in the travel tool.
